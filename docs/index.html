Method and emission factors

N₂O-N is converted to N₂O with 44/28 and to CO2-equivalent with a 100-year GWP of 273 (IPCC AR6). Indirect N₂O covers volatilisation (FracGASF × EF4) and leaching (FracLEACH × EF5). The urea switch assumes the replacement fertilizer has the same manufacturing footprint per kg N. Soil carbon, crop residues and manure are not included.
